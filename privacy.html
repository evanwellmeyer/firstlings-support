Privacy Policy

Firstlings

Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE]

Firstlings is built to keep your family's information private. In short: we don't collect it. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. What you enter is stored on your device and is never sent to us. Device backups, and anything you choose to export or share, follow your own settings.

What you enter, and where it lives

When you use Firstlings, you may enter:

  • Your child's name or nickname, birth date, and due date
  • Which activities you tried, skipped, or saved
  • Milestones you log, with dates, notes, and photos you choose to add
  • Monthly-birthday photos and notes you choose to add
  • Journal entries you write, such as memories and questions, with any photos you add
  • Belly photos and notes you choose to add while expecting

This information is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is not sent to us or anyone else. We cannot see it, and we don't have a copy.

Device backups

If your device is set to back up to iCloud or to a computer, your device's backup may include the app's data, as with most apps. Those backups are handled by Apple or your computer under your settings and Apple's privacy policy, not by us.

Backup files and photo export

If you tap "Back up everything" in Settings, Firstlings creates one file on your device with all of your children's details, notes, moments, and photos. You choose where it goes (for example Files, iCloud Drive, or AirDrop), and it is never sent to us. The file isn't encrypted, so anyone who has it can read it; keep it somewhere private. Restoring reads a backup file you choose, on your device.

If you tap "Save all photos," Firstlings prepares copies of your saved photos for you to save to your photo library, to Files, or to share. If you save them to your photo library, iOS asks first, and Firstlings can only add photos, not see your library.

Photos

When you add a photo to a moment, you choose it with Apple's photo picker. Firstlings only receives the photo you pick, not your whole library. A smaller copy is saved in the app on your device.

Reminders and your calendar

If you turn on daily, monthly-birthday, or belly photo reminders, they are scheduled on your device by iOS. Nothing is sent to a server.

If you tap "Add to Calendar" for monthly birthdays, Firstlings opens Apple's own add-event screen. You decide whether to save the event, and it's stored in your calendar like any other event. Firstlings can't read your calendar.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. We don't receive your name, payment details, or Apple ID. The app checks your purchase status, and whether you joined during a free launch period, using information Apple stores on your device.

Links

Resource links open in your web browser. Once you leave the app, the website's own privacy policy applies.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, sell, rent, or share personal information.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools, and we don't track you across apps or websites.
  • We don't have servers that store your information.

Children's privacy

Firstlings is meant for parents and caregivers, not for children to use. Information about a child that a parent enters stays on the parent's device and is never collected by us.

Deleting your information

You can delete a child, along with their moments, notes, and photos, from Settings → Children. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Device backups and Firstlings backup files you've made are managed by you, in your device or iCloud settings or wherever you saved them.

Changes

If we change how Firstlings handles information, we'll update this policy and let you know in the app before the change applies.
