Firstlings

Play, notice, and remember

Firstlings is a quiet companion for the years from pregnancy to about age 3½: small ways to play together, a place to notice what's new, and a story you keep as you go. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, and everything you save stays on your phone.

Get in touch

Questions, bugs, or ideas? Email wellmeyerapps.support@gmail.com. It helps to include your iPhone model and iOS version. You can also tap Send feedback in the app's Settings.

Your information

Where is my information stored?

Only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage. We don't have servers that store it, and we never receive it.

How do I back up, or move to a new phone?

In Settings, under Backup, tap Back up everything. Firstlings makes one file with every child, note, and photo. Save it somewhere safe, like iCloud Drive or a computer. On a new phone, tap Restore from a backup on the welcome screen (or in Settings) and choose the file. An iPhone backup or transfer also brings Firstlings along.

The backup file isn't encrypted, so keep it somewhere private.

How do I get my photos out?

In Settings, under Backup, tap Save all photos. You can save them to your photo library or to Files. Each photo keeps the date it belongs to.

How do I delete my information?

Open Settings, tap a child, and delete them to remove their moments, notes, and photos. Deleting the app removes everything from your phone. Backup files you've saved are yours to delete wherever you keep them.

Using Firstlings

Belly photos and monthly photos

While you're expecting, the This week page has a spot for a belly photo each week, and Our story keeps them week by week. After your baby arrives, each monthly birthday has a spot for a photo. All of them are optional.

Reminders aren't arriving.

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. Check that they're turned on in the app's Settings, and that notifications for Firstlings are allowed in the iPhone Settings app. Reminders pause for a paused pregnancy profile.

Does Firstlings tell me whether my child is on track?

No. Firstlings is for information and play. It is not a screening or diagnostic tool, and it doesn't assess your child or your pregnancy. If you have a question about your child's health or development, or your own, please talk with your doctor or care team.

Firstlings Plus

Is Plus free?

For now, yes. While Firstlings is new, Plus is free for everyone, and families who join during this time keep it for good.

I bought Plus. How do I restore it?

In Settings, under Firstlings Plus, tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID. Subscriptions are managed in the iPhone Settings app under your name, then Subscriptions.
